Who We Are

How we got started

Palmeras Home Services began as one family doing right by their neighbors — a single home, cleaned properly, one job at a time. Word got around, and that same hands-on approach is still how we run things today, whether we're in a living room or a lobby.

Our approach

We clean with natural, non-toxic products that are safe around kids and pets, and we treat every space like it's our own. No shortcuts, no rushed jobs — just a clean, healthy environment you can actually feel good about.
